AR Rating is how many points better than an average FBS team a club is on a neutral field, from every score this season adjusted for opponent and site. Built from Game Scores: opponent strength, margin, home or road, and a bonus for winning. A preseason projection from last season, returning production and roster talent fades as games are played; no human polls are used. Game data and lines: CollegeFootballData.com. Team logos appear when the site runs on its own host.
